# HA Shopping List

A comprehensive shopping list app for Home Assistant with recipe management and category organization.

## Features

- 📝 Add items with custom quantities and categories
- 🏷️ Automatic organization by store aisle/section  
- ✅ Check off items to move to cart (crossed out at bottom)
- 🛒 Checkout confirmation to remove all cart items
- 🍳 Create and manage recipes with ingredients
- ➕ Add entire recipes to shopping list with one click
- 🎨 Automatically matches your Home Assistant theme

## Quick Start

1. After installation, go to **Settings** → **Devices & Services**
2. Click **+ Add Integration**
3. Search for "HA Shopping List"
4. The shopping list panel will appear in your sidebar

## Usage

### Shopping List
- Enter item name, quantity (e.g., "2", "5L", "1lb"), and category
- Items are automatically grouped by category
- Check items to move them to the "In Cart" section
- Click "Checkout" to remove all checked items

### Recipes
- Switch to the Recipes tab
- Create recipes with all ingredients predefined
- Click "Add to List" to add all recipe ingredients to your shopping list
